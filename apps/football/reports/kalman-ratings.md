# MarketScope Ratings: Kalman unit ratings (MS-030)

_Generated by `marketscope_football.ratings.run_eval` on 2026-10-18. Parameters fitted by `ratings.fit` on 2016–22 only and frozen in `ratings/params.json`; 2023–24 never informed a choice; 2025 untouched._

## Model

- **Channels (separate filters):** pass EPA/dropback, rush EPA/rush, success rate (all luck-adjusted, capped, WP-weighted; ADR-029), points per drive, and net special-teams EPA.
- **State:** per channel, 32 offense + 32 defense units and a league mean (special teams: 32 units). Each team-game observes `mean + off[A] + def[B] ± hfa/2`, noise σ²/n_eff, so opponent adjustment happens inside the filter.
- **Time:** random walk between weeks; at a season boundary unit → ρ·unit + noise, ρ depending on whether the unit's play-caller changed; a mid-season play-caller change adds one-time variance.
- **Game layer:** margin = Σ w·feature + c·(not neutral), OLS on the filter's pre-week predictions for 2016–22. Win probability Φ(margin / σ) as for the benchmarks.
- **Burn-in:** the filter starts in 2012; 2012–15 never enter the likelihood.
