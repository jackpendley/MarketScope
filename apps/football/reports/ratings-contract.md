## Luck model (fitted on 2016–22)

- Expected wins: logit P(win) = 0.604 × home + 0.291 × net adjusted EPA (1,897 games). One extra point of net EPA is worth about 7.3 percentage points of win probability near 50%.
- Pythagorean exponent: 2.79 (224 team-seasons). The classic NFL value is 2.37; higher-scoring modern seasons push it up.
- **Home teams win more than their efficiency implies.** Margin ≈ 0.70 × net adjusted EPA +1.40 points (2016–22). The 1.4-point home term is outside scrimmage EPA (it is consistent with penalties, whose plays the channel excludes, and other non-scrimmage effects), so expected wins keep a home term instead of calling it luck.
- Check: league-wide luck sums to zero in every snapshot (max |sum| 1.5e-14).
