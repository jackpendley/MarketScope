## Reading this

- **Out means out**, and **Doubtful almost always does too**: under 1% of Doubtful players play in either era, far below the ≈ 7% that ELWAY assumes for quarterbacks (our QB sample: 0 of 39).
- **Questionable is stable overall** (≈ 68% then, ≈ 66% now) **but the practice signal has weakened.** In 2016–22 a full Friday practice meant 85% and a missed one 41%; in 2023–25 those are 71% and 52%. A model fit only on 2016–22 would be overconfident about practice status today.
- Consequence for MS-031: P(plays) is modelled from status × practice with the practice effect allowed to drift (estimated on a rolling window of seasons before the prediction), shrunk across position groups, and checked on 2023–24 validation. Never tuned on the 2025 test season.
- Quarterbacks listed Questionable play less often (53%) than other positions, but the sample is small (n = 219) and the interval wide.
- Limitation: "played" is any snap. A Questionable player who plays may still play less than usual; snap-share effects are modelled in MS-031.
