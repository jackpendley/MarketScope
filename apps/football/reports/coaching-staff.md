# Coaching staff and play-callers

Generated by `python -m marketscope_football.coaching_build --report` from `data/coaching_staff.csv` (ADR-031). Counts are team-weeks, 2016–2026 (2026 through the scheduled end of the regular season).

## How each play-caller is supported

| Basis     | Offense | Defense | Meaning                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| list      | 51%     | 0%      | named in a season-wide list of every team's play-caller (ESPN 2023–26, 4for4 2020, partial 2016/2018 lists) |
| direct    | 9%      | 7%      | a source about that coach and season (or the dated change)                                                  |
| bracketed | 7%      | 0%      | same coach and team named in lists before and after, no reported change in between                          |
| stint     | 2%      | 3%      | a source covering the coach's tenure, not that exact season                                                 |
| rule      | 31%     | 90%     | no source: the coordinator for that side (or the head coach when there is none)                             |

Defensive play-calling is rarely reported team by team, so most defensive weeks rest on the rule. Known exceptions (head coaches or untitled assistants calling the defense) are sourced overrides.

## Head-coach source

Head coaches come from nflverse schedules (`home_coach`/`away_coach`), which give the exact week of a change, except where nflverse is stale: it does not record several interim head coaches (e.g. 2016 LA, 2019 CAR, 2024 CHI/NO/NYJ, 2025 NYG/TEN), and for the season in progress it still shows the previous season's coaches. There the Wikipedia staff page and dated changes in `coordinator_changes.csv` are used.
