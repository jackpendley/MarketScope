# Season simulation: evaluation (MS-034)

Generated by `ratings/run_sim_eval.py` with ratings-v1 (frozen parameters), 2,000 simulated seasons per projection. Backtest seasons 2016-2024; the 2025 test season is not used. Nothing in the simulation is tuned on these results. Model estimates, not betting advice.

## Game noise

The key-number margin distribution was fitted on model errors (scale 13.05 points), which include rating error. Over 1,906 games in 2016-2022 the rating-error variance of a matchup averaged 10.55 points², so the simulation plays games with scale 12.64 and draws each team's rating error once per simulated season (correlated across its games) plus a weekly drift.

## Tiebreakers

NFL procedures through strength of schedule, then a coin flip. On the real results they reproduce every playoff field and Wild Card matchup of 2016-24 (`tests/test_simulate.py`).

## Brier scores (lower is better)

Projections before week 1 and after each regular-season week, averaged over teams and weeks in each bucket. Seasons already decided (all games played) are excluded.
